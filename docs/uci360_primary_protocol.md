## Why This Protocol Exists

The Measurement submission (MEAS-D-26-16493) was desk-rejected without review.
The post-rejection audit identified a structural error in the evidence
hierarchy of the rejected study, not merely a presentation problem:

- The confirmation corpus (Wörner et al. 2025) carries **two nominal
  concentration levels per analyte** (Diacetyl 0.1/1.0 ppm; Phenylethanol
  200/1000 ppm) and **6–12 held-out test rows per sequence**. A recalibration
  claim cannot establish linearity, working range, or the separation of
  sensitivity change from offset change on two levels.
- The corpus that was demoted to "exploratory", UCI 360 (De Vito Air Quality,
  DOI `10.24432/C59K5F`), carries **90/238/751 distinct reference values** for
  CO/NO2/NOx, **343–484 held-out test rows per sequence**, and target values
  that are **co-located certified reference analyser readings** rather than
  supplied nominal labels.

The original Kill Test returned FAIL because it required same-direction joint
hits in **two** corpora (`minimum_supporting_corpora: 2`). UCI 360 produced 4
joint hits; UCI 270 produced 0. The verdict was therefore driven by the weaker
corpus. That gate was written for the **retired** claim (lightweight
recalibration is broadly effective). It was never re-specified after the
research question changed to "when is lightweight updating insufficient?".

This protocol promotes UCI 360 to primary confirmation corpus and re-specifies
the gates for the current question.

## Disclosure of Hypothesis Provenance

This is the central integrity constraint of this protocol and must be stated in
the manuscript.

The decision-rule hypothesis in Section "Pre-Specified Hypotheses" below was
**generated by inspecting** the earlier 3-window UCI 360 exploratory run
(`results/kill_test_uci360_v1`), which used windows 1–2 as source and window 3
as target over the first 270 days. In that run, lightweight output calibration
improved held-out nRMSE in 9 of 9 analyte/model cells at the 5 % budget, and the
absolute gain tracked the frozen model's degradation (NOx: relative increase
2.37–2.61, gain ≈ −50 %; NO2: relative increase 0.09–0.30, gain ≈ 0 %).

The hypothesis is therefore **exploratory in origin**. It is not presented as
pre-registered from project start. This protocol pre-specifies it before the
re-windowed benchmark is executed, and the evaluation separates:

- **Rule-fitting windows** (target windows 4–9): overlap the 270-day span that
  generated the hypothesis. Results here are *in-sample for hypothesis
  generation* and are reported as such.
- **Held-out windows** (target windows 10–13): cover 2004-12-05 to 2005-04-04,
  i.e. **121 days never used in any prior analysis in this project**. This is
  the confirmation evidence for the decision rule.

No threshold, window boundary, or gate defined below may be changed after the
held-out windows are inspected.

## The Operational Gap This Protocol Closes

`frozen_nrmse_relative_increase` in the existing implementation is computed
from **test-set** error. It is not observable at deployment time, so it cannot
drive an operational decision. The rejected manuscript reported it as a
descriptive stratifier and therefore produced no decision rule.

This protocol defines a **reference-panel diagnostic** computed only from
quantities a measurement team actually holds before deciding:

```
D_ref(N) = ( nRMSE_frozen(reference panel of size N)
             - nRMSE_source_validation )
           / nRMSE_source_validation
```

- `nRMSE_frozen(reference panel)`: the deployed source model scored on the N
  labelled target-period references.
- `nRMSE_source_validation`: the same model family scored on the latest source
  window held back from its own training, i.e. the existing
  `preceding_source_error`.

Both terms are available without touching the held-out test set. `D_ref` is
therefore a legitimate decision input; the existing test-set quantity is not.

## Pre-Specified Hypotheses

**H1 (diagnostic validity).** Across the 30 sequences, `D_ref(5)` is positively
associated with the absolute benefit of lightweight output calibration,
`ΔnRMSE = nRMSE_frozen(test) − nRMSE_calibrator_update(test)`.
Pre-specified test: Spearman rank correlation, reported separately for
rule-fitting and held-out windows, with the sequence as the unit.

**H2 (rule utility).** A decision rule of the form

```
if D_ref(N) >= tau:  recalibrate (calibrator_update)
else:                keep frozen
```

achieves lower mean test nRMSE than **both** always-frozen and
always-recalibrate on the held-out windows.

H2 is the operational claim. H1 without H2 is descriptive only.

**H3 (budget for the decision, not for the fit).** The reference count needed
for `D_ref(N)` to select the same action as `D_ref(N_max)` is smaller than the
count needed for `calibrator_update` to reach its own performance plateau. In
words: deciding whether to recalibrate is cheaper than recalibrating well.

## Pre-Specified Evaluation

Baselines, all scored as mean test nRMSE over sequences:

- `always_frozen`
- `always_recalibrate`
- `rule(tau, N)`
- `oracle_action`: per sequence, the better of frozen and recalibrate chosen
  with test-set knowledge. Upper bound, not attainable.

Threshold grid, frozen now: `tau in {0.10, 0.20, 0.30, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00}`.
Decision budgets, frozen now: `N in {2, 5, 10, 20}`.

`tau` and `N` are selected on rule-fitting windows 4–9 only, then applied
unchanged to held-out windows 10–13. Selecting them on the held-out windows is
prohibited.

## Pre-Specified Verdicts

- **H1 supported** if Spearman rho > 0 with p < 0.05 on held-out windows.
- **H2 supported** if `rule` beats both `always_frozen` and
  `always_recalibrate` on held-out mean test nRMSE, using the `tau`/`N` chosen
  on rule-fitting windows.
- **H2 refuted** if `always_recalibrate` is at least as good as `rule`. This is
  a publishable outcome: it would mean lightweight calibration on a
  reference-analyser corpus is reliable enough that no gate is needed, which
  contradicts the heterogeneity seen on the two-level Wörner corpus and locates
  the boundary at corpus quality rather than at reference count.
- **H3 supported** if action agreement with `D_ref(N_max)` reaches 100 % at an
  N strictly smaller than the smallest N at which `calibrator_update` is within
  5 % of its plateau nRMSE.

All three outcomes are reportable. No verdict may be revised after inspection.

## Role of the Two Retired Corpora

- **Wörner et al. 2025** is retained as a **contrast corpus**, reported for what
  it can support: discrimination between two nominal concentration levels under
  temporal shift. It is not presented as concentration-regression confirmation.
  Its 12-improve / 12-worsen split at the 5-reference budget is the informative
  contrast against UCI 360.
- **UCI 270** remains exploratory. Its batch-level-only time resolution and
  9–20 test rows per sequence do not meet the standard applied here.
